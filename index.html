How the data reaches EasyReplenish — Option 1: FTP transfer
  1. A scheduled job in SAP writes the day's extracts as flat files.
  2. The files are placed on a secure FTP location, controlled by Eveready's IT team.
  3. EasyReplenish collects the files from that location each night.
  4. Each file is checked — record counts, dates and product codes — before anything is loaded.
  5. If a file fails the check, it is reported back the same morning and nothing part-loaded.
SAP stays the single source of truth. EasyReplenish only reads from it — no SAP data is changed, and no master data is re-created on our side. Approved forecasts can be sent back into SAP later if Eveready wants that.

How will EasyReplenish forecast for Eveready

A two-slide summary — business explanation, then technical detail.

Built from your SAP data
Recent sales Same season, last year Festivals & sale days New / popular / slow-moving
Two methods, for two different businesses

Batteries — a steady, trend-based method

  • High volume — even a small forecast error means a lot of excess or shortfall stock
  • Demand varies by zone — driven by local conditions and channel mix
  • Different baseline demand across zones
  • A simple trend-based method gets this right consistently — manual effort doesn't

Lighting — a smarter, event-aware method

  • Close to 500 products, with new ones launching often
  • Sourced from China — long wait if the forecast is wrong
  • Demand jumps sharply around festivals like Diwali
  • New products have no sales history to learn from
Segment before picking a model
Batteries — mature demand Lighting — fast-fashion-adjacent
